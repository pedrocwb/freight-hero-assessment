id: escalation
title: Escalation to the Hero team
description: When to create a Hero task, when it is urgent, and what to write in it.
---

The Hero team is our operations team. They make phone calls and handle what we
cannot do by message. A Hero task hands a case to them.

Create a Hero task when:
1. A procedure says so, for example when the driver does not answer the last ETA request.
2. Someone asks something that the load details do not answer.
3. Someone asks for something only a person can do: a phone call, an appointment
   change, a question about rates or pay.
4. A message reports a problem: an accident, a breakdown, a refused or damaged load,
   a missed appointment.
5. A message is unclear, or no procedure covers it. Do not improvise.

Mark the task urgent when a person must act now: the truck may miss its appointment
(for example, the delay risk is high and the driver does not answer), someone may be
hurt, or the load may be lost or damaged. Otherwise the task is not urgent.

What to write in the task:
- Title: a short instruction, like "Call the driver for the ETA".
- Description, in this order:
  1. The load number and the stop it is about.
  2. What happened, with local times.
  3. What was already tried, for example "ETA asked by SMS at 07:01 and 07:31, no answer".
  4. What the Hero should do, and what to report back.

Create one task per problem. If the recent events already show a task for the same
problem, do not create another one.

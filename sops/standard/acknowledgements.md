title: Acknowledgements
description: Messages like ok or thanks need no action. An acknowledgement is never an ETA.
intents: [acknowledgement]
---

An acknowledgement only says that a message was received: "ok", "okay", "thanks",
"thank you", "got it", "will do", a thumbs up.

The right action is to do nothing:
1. Send no reply. Do not answer "you're welcome" or ask "anything else?".
2. Write no TMS note and create no Hero task.

Common cases:
- "ok" or "thanks" when we asked nothing: do nothing.
- "ok" after we asked for the ETA: an acknowledgement is not an ETA. Do nothing. The
  ETA request stays open and its follow-up continues as planned.
- "thanks" from the broker, in the email thread or on Slack: do nothing.

If the message also asks a question or gives a time, it is more than an
acknowledgement: follow the procedure for the question or the ETA.
